Solutions

Different organisations need different answers from the same fire. Here's how our forecasting and analytics capabilities map to the decisions your team actually makes.

Fire Agencies

Operational Fire Intelligence

Give incident controllers and duty officers the temporal resolution that daily fire danger ratings can't provide — so crews and aircraft move before conditions turn, not after.

  • Sub-hourly fire potential from live weather station feeds
  • Escalation probability given ignition, from 0.1 to 100 ha
  • Threshold alerting by API, dashboard, SMS, or email
  • Warning thresholds set in cost–loss terms against your response costs

Forestry & Land Management

Estate-Scale Fire Risk Planning

Equip forestry managers, regional councils, and land management agencies with the analysis to prioritise fuel treatment, plan suppression capacity, and defend high-value stands.

  • Landscape versus weather control on escalation, by fire size
  • Which environments produce fires that escape suppression
  • Seasonal shift, with spring growth probability rising toward summer levels
  • 5 km projections of fire-growth probability for long-horizon estate planning

Wildfire Exposure for Critical Assets

Help network operators, transport corridors, and telecommunications providers understand where wildfire threatens assets and when conditions warrant operational restrictions.

  • Spatially explicit fire risk at asset and corridor level

Quantified Wildfire Exposure

Provide underwriters, actuaries, and risk analysts with transparent wildfire hazard models for pricing, portfolio management, and climate-risk disclosure.

  • Projected change under SSP1-2.6 to SSP5-8.5, with stated uncertainty
  • API-delivered risk layers for portfolio integration
  • Transparent, peer-reviewed methodology

Research & Government

Evidence for National Fire Strategy

Partner with universities, Crown Research Institutes, and policy agencies to turn fire records into the quantitative evidence base that strategy and funding decisions need.

  • National 5 km projections via delta-ALE spatial transfer learning
  • Scale-dependent driver attribution across fire-size thresholds
  • Collaborative R&D, co-publication, and peer review
  • Reproducible pipelines and interactive data viewers
