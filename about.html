Our Story

About Wildfire Intelligence

We exist to close the gap between the fire data organisations already hold and the decisions that depend on it.

Who We Are

Fire science, machine learning, and software — applied to the fires you actually fight

Wildfire Intelligence was founded on a simple observation: the people making high-stakes fire decisions are often working with tools that update once a day, while the fire environment changes by the hour.

We close that gap. We combine fire science, machine learning, geospatial modelling, and software engineering to build systems that are scientifically defensible, operationally practical, and designed to run in the environments our clients actually work in.

We work alongside research and operational partners — including the University of Canterbury, Scion (New Zealand Forest Research Institute), and Covey Associates in Queensland — on projects across New Zealand, Australia, and Latin America.

Scientifically Rigorous

Our methods are grounded in peer-reviewed fire science and validated against decades of real fire records. We publish what we build.

Operationally Practical

Research that stays in a paper doesn't help a duty officer at 2pm on a bad day. We design for deployment, not demonstration.

Transparent by Default

No black boxes where accountability matters. We document assumptions, show validation, and explain what a model can't tell you.

Research to Product

We turn scientific outputs into operational tools — from prototype to production, from journal paper to deployed dashboard.

Alberto Ardid

Founder & CEO

Alberto founded Wildfire Intelligence to put research-grade fire modelling into the hands of the people making operational decisions. He leads the company's science and engineering, and works directly on client projects rather than at arm's length from them.

His research spans fire-potential forecasting, the drivers of fire escalation, and how fire growth responds to a changing climate — published as peer-reviewed work with collaborators at the University of Canterbury, Scion, and Covey Associates. The three studies on our case studies page are his; the methods behind every product we sell are in the open literature, which is deliberate.

He is based in Christchurch and works across New Zealand, Australia, and Latin America.

Our Expertise

Where our capabilities intersect

We're not a pure software shop, and we're not a pure research lab. We sit at the intersection, and that's where the most useful fire work happens.

Fire Science

Fire weather, fuel dynamics, ignition and spread processes, and suppression practice. We understand the physics behind the data.

AI & Machine Learning

Supervised forecasting, unsupervised classification, and uncertainty quantification applied to continuous environmental time series.

Geospatial Modelling

Gridded projections, spatial statistics, remote sensing, and terrain analysis — national coverage down to asset-level resolution.

Software Engineering

Full-stack development, API design, automated data pipelines, and deployment. We build production systems, not prototypes to rebuild later.
