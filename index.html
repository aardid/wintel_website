What We Do

Forecast the potential, quantify the value

Four connected capabilities, each built and published as peer-reviewed method rather than a black box: forecast the near term, explain what drives escalation, project it forward, and price the decision.

Sub-Hourly Potential Forecasting

Machine learning on live automatic weather station feeds, updating fire potential between the daily fire-danger cycles that operations run on today.

Escalation Probability by Fire Size

The probability a fire exceeds 0.1, 1, 10, or 100 ha given ignition — and which fuel, terrain, and weather conditions govern each of those thresholds.

Climate-Change Projections

Spatial transfer learning that carries a trained fire model onto 5 km CMIP6 climate grids, mapping where fire-growth probability rises, by how much, and driven by what.

Forecast Value, Not Just Skill

Cost–loss analysis and potential economic value: at which warning thresholds a forecast actually pays for the organisation acting on it.
